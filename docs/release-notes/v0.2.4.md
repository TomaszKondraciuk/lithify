A speaker setting for mono: with a single speaker, Spotify through Lithify plays all of the music.

## Changed

- **Settings → Basics → Channels** on the speaker's page offers stereo or mono, picked from two
  pictures. Mono mixes the left and the right channel into both outputs.
- Why: the speaker's amplifier board has a Stereo/Mono switch. On stereo, a single speaker plays
  only the left channel, and the right one goes to the output for a second speaker. With mono,
  the speaker plays both.
- The mixing loses nothing audible: each sample is the exact average of the two channels, or half
  a step of 16 bits below it, which is less than the noise already there.
- Cast, AirPlay and Bluetooth still follow the switch on the speaker.

## librespot

Unchanged: the same commit of its development branch as in 0.2.3 (99b6088), built with the newest
compatible versions of its libraries.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps
its settings and its Spotify login; stereo stays the default.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0, one speaker set to mono.
  Playback was checked, and the mixing was measured on a million samples.
- Computers: `lithify build` now packs Lithify's own ALSA definitions into the bundle. Its tests
  pass on Windows, macOS and Linux.
