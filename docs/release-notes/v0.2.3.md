The speaker now keeps what passes between it and its amplifier board, and a restart no longer
takes that along.

## Changed

- **Logs → amplifier board** on the speaker's page shows the messages between the speaker and
  its amplifier board, which has a controller of its own. They are kept on the speaker, so they
  survive a restart. On 2026-10-29 the board switched to its RCA input (a solid purple light):
  Lithify played on, but nothing was heard over Wi-Fi until the speaker restarted.
  Should that happen again, these messages show when it happened and what the board said.

## librespot

Unchanged: the same commit of its development branch as in 0.2.2 (99b6088), built with the newest
compatible versions of its libraries.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps
its settings and its Spotify login.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0. Tested with the speaker
  restarted, and with only Lithify restarted.
- Computers: this release changes nothing on the computer. Its tests pass on Windows, macOS and
  Linux.
