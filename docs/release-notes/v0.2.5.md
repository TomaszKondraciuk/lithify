Picking a podcast episode in a playlist now plays that episode, and the log on the speaker's page
shows far less noise.

## Fixed

- **An episode in a playlist plays.** In a playlist that mixes podcast episodes with songs,
  picking an episode in the Spotify app started the playlist's first song. Lithify now loads a
  playlist from a Spotify service that lists every item, episodes included.
- **A Spotify Jam no longer floods the log.** While your account takes part in a Jam, every pause
  of a member added a long warning to the log that named all the members. One evening of a Jam
  filled almost the whole log and pushed days of history out of it. Such a message is now one
  short line, left out of the normal log.

## Changed

- The log on the speaker's page keeps a line up to 1 KB, so a burst of long messages can no
  longer push the history out. Long runs of letters and digits show as `[hidden]`.
- The log view no longer shows a lost connection to Spotify in red once Lithify has connected
  again, the same way *Run a check* has counted it since 0.2.2.

## librespot

The same commit of its development branch as in 0.2.4 (99b6088), with two more local patches:
`playlist-episodes` and `quiet-session-update`. Built with the newest compatible versions of its
libraries.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps
its settings and its Spotify login.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0. From the iOS app, a
  podcast episode in a public playlist that mixes episodes and songs played, and so did a song
  from the same playlist.
- Computers: this release changes nothing on the computer. Its tests pass on Windows, macOS and
  Linux.
