The speaker now picks Spotify's audio servers by measuring them, instead of skipping a fixed list.

## Changed

- **Audio servers picked by measurement.** For every track Spotify gives the speaker a list of its
  audio servers (CDNs), and the speaker tries them in order, waiting up to 10 seconds on one that
  does not answer. Every 30 minutes Lithify now connects to each of them a few times and skips
  the ones that do not answer or are much slower than the best. The best one is never skipped.
  A change waits for a second measurement that agrees, two minutes later, and when librespot gives
  up on a server, a measurement starts at once.
- The *Network* card on the speaker's page shows the last measurement, for example "Akamai 16 ms ·
  Cloudflare 7 ms · Fastly 20 ms".
- **Settings → Advanced → Network → Audio servers (CDN)**: "pick by measurement" (the default) or
  "skip only those listed below". The servers on that list are skipped either way.

## librespot

Unchanged: the same commit of its development branch as in 0.2.5 (99b6088) with the same four
local patches, built with the newest compatible versions of its libraries.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps its
settings and its Spotify login. Servers you added to *CDN servers to skip* stay skipped; empty that
list to let the measurement decide about them too.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0. The three CDNs that answer
  today were measured; one cut off by hand was skipped at once, and used again two measurements
  after it came back.
- Computers: this release changes nothing on the computer. Its tests pass on Windows, macOS and
  Linux.
