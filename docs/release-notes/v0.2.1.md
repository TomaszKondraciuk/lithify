The speaker now rides out an internet outage by itself, and librespot is on the newest commit of
its development branch.

## Fixed

- **After an internet outage** the speaker waits until Spotify answers again, then connects by
  itself. Before, it restarted librespot over and over while the internet was down, and the page
  said "librespot keeps exiting right after it starts"; right after an update, that could even
  have rolled the new version back.
- **Fewer needless restarts:** a connection to Spotify that comes back by itself within a second
  no longer gets librespot restarted.
- **A lost connection librespot kept quiet about** (Spotify's server stopped answering) is now
  noticed and repaired like the others.
- *Run a check* on the page no longer counts a dropped connection to Spotify as the sound running
  dry, and the page's log dims more of the harmless messages.
- The page's log shortens your Spotify account name in every line, as it did in the sign-in line.

## librespot

librespot moves to the newest commit of its development branch (99b6088). With it, picking a
track in the middle of a playlist plays that track instead of the first one, and a malformed
request from another device on the network can no longer stop librespot.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps
its settings and its Spotify login.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0, including an internet
  outage simulated on the speaker (its local network stayed up).
- Computers: this release changes only `lithify status` on the computer; its tests pass on
  Windows, macOS and Linux.
