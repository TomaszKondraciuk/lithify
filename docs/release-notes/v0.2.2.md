The speaker's page now tells Lithify's own repairs apart from real problems, and after a restart
it says how long the speaker was down.

## Changed

- *Run a check* on the page no longer counts a lost connection to Spotify that Lithify
  restored by itself as an error. Spotify's servers close the connection a few times a day, and
  Lithify connects again within seconds. After ordinary days the check no longer turns yellow.
- **After a restart**, *Recent events* on the page say how long the speaker was down, to within
  5 minutes. About a minute means the speaker restarted; longer means it had no power. To know
  this, Lithify notes every 5 minutes that it is running.

## librespot

Unchanged: the same commit of its development branch as in 0.2.1 (99b6088), built with the newest
compatible versions of its libraries.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps
its settings and its Spotify login.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0. Tested with the speaker
  restarted, and with only Lithify restarted.
- Computers: this release changes nothing on the computer. Its tests pass on Windows, macOS and
  Linux.
