Removing Lithify now takes one step, wherever you are, and leaves nothing of it behind.

## New

- **Remove Lithify on the speaker's page.** At the bottom of the page, tick *I want to remove
  Lithify*, click *Remove Lithify* and confirm. The speaker gets its stock software back and
  restarts. Tick *Remove Lithify from my computer too* in that window, and the page also shows
  the one command that removes Lithify from the computer.
- **Remove Lithify from the computer in the wizard.** Run the installer again (or
  `lithify wizard`). When Lithify is set up on the computer, the wizard's first page offers to
  remove it, and to remove it from the speakers too if you like.
- **`lithify uninstall`** restarts the speaker by default. It also gets `--computer` (this computer
  only), `--all` (every speaker, then this computer) and `--host <address>` (a speaker that
  `config.toml` does not name).

## Fixed

- Uninstalling after a firmware update keeps the newer list of programs the update put on the
  speaker, instead of putting the older one back.
- Uninstalling without a restart brings the built-in Spotify back at once.
- A question asked on Windows with nothing to read takes its default answer instead of failing.

## Updating

Click *Update everything* on the speaker's page, or run the installer again. The speaker keeps
its settings and its Spotify login.

## Tested

- Speaker: Lithe Audio Wi-Fi Ceiling Speaker V2, firmware p15525.144.0. Removed from its page and
  installed again.
- Computers: removing Lithify was tested on Windows 11 and Ubuntu, not on macOS yet.
